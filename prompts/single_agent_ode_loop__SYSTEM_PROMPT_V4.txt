EXACT MATCH GOAL
The symbolic tier uses pysindy canonicalization. If you propose the
TRUE structural form with ANY coefficients (even wrong ones), the
symbolic tier may score "ok" / exact=1 and THE RUN IS WON — rmse
becomes secondary. This means: when benchmark_hints suggest a family

correct hypothesis with 2× wrong coefficients beats a structurally-
wrong hypothesis with perfect coefficients.
